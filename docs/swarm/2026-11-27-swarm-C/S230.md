# S230 — Principles page layout

Rerun of S215. Maciej asked for a less cluttered principles page and said this page does not need new tests. Layout only. The core manifest and the digest were not edited. `LICENSE` is byte-identical to master.

## What changed

- `v2/src/principles.njk` — the page structure.
- `v2/src/assets/site.css` — styles used only by this page.
- This note.

The site is the Eleventy build in `v2/` (`npm run build`). The principles page is `v2/src/principles.njk`, published at `/principles/`.

## Layout

- The four sentences that opened the page are summary cards under the title: without agendas; source, date, and who said it; disagreement shown side by side; saying when we do not know.
- An "On this page" list sits beside those cards from 960px up. On a phone, and on a tablet portrait, the cards stack and the list follows them.
- Each long section is a native `<details>` element, closed until it is opened. There is no new script.
- Card and contents links point at an anchor inside the closed text, so the browser opens that section. The heading stays below the sticky bar.
- "Record of changes" is the last section, after "Where these rules are written".

## Words

The rule and policy sentences are the previous sentences. The opening paragraph was split into those four cards on the sentence boundaries, and the section headings are repeated in the contents list. Two labels were added for the layout: "Core principles" and "On this page". No rule was rewritten.

## Check

`npm run build` in `v2/` succeeded. In Chrome, at 390×844, 768×1024, 1280×800, and 1440×900:

- No horizontal overflow.
- The contents list is beside the cards on the laptop widths, and below them on the phone and the 768px tablet.
- A card and a contents link each open the matching section, with the heading visible under the sticky bar.
- The revision record is after "Where these rules are written".

No new test was added for this page.
