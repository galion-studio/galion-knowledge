HORBM layered architecture: one turn, both ways

A conversationalist first, grounded in sourced truth

Heuristic Open Retrieval Brain Models. The twelve layers sit in the middle. The left path is your words going down, in the order the turn really reaches each layer. The right path is the reply coming back up. They are not mirror images: the lookup in 7 and 8 runs before the dialogue manager picks a move, because the move filter needs the evidence, and the reply is checked by safety, persona and empathy on the way up. Numbers like (12) match the notes on the full plate. As of 19 Nov 2026, Europe/Zagreb.
